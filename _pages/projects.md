This page highlights selected research and technical projects related to computer vision, deep learning, marine robotics, autonomous systems, and multimodal sensing.

## Selected Research Projects

### Vision-Based Significant Wave Height Estimation

Developing deep learning methods to estimate significant wave height from coastal webcam imagery. The work investigates visual feature learning, regression under highly imbalanced sea-state distributions, and improved estimation of rare and high-wave conditions.

**Topics:** Computer Vision · Deep Learning · Regression · Ocean Sensing · Imbalanced Learning

---

### Polarization-Aware Wave Perception

Developing learning-based methods for significant wave height estimation from polarimetric imagery. The project investigates polarization-aware representations, multi-scale visual features, and efficient deep learning architectures for wave sensing.

**Topics:** Polarimetric Imaging · Vision Transformers · Deep Learning · Ocean Engineering

---

### Multimodal Wave Tank Perception

Developing a controlled experimental framework for collecting synchronized multimodal data in a wave-tank environment.

The sensing system is designed around complementary measurements from cameras, LiDAR, IMU, GPS, sonar, and wave-measurement instrumentation to support wave-state estimation and marine perception research.

**Topics:** Multimodal Sensing · LiDAR · Camera · IMU · Sensor Fusion · Experimental Robotics

---

### Marine Robotics Perception

Exploring perception methods for autonomous surface vehicles operating in dynamic marine environments.

The project focuses on environmental understanding using visual sensing, 3D perception, sensor fusion, and learning-based perception methods.

**Topics:** Marine Robotics · Autonomous Systems · Computer Vision · LiDAR · Sensor Fusion

## Technical Learning Projects

### LiDAR Perception for Robotics

Developing foundational and practical skills in LiDAR sensing, point-cloud processing, 3D perception, and applications to autonomous robotic systems.

---

### Sensor Fusion and State Estimation

Studying filtering and sensor-fusion methods for robotics, including Kalman filtering, IMU-based estimation, and multimodal state estimation.

---

### Machine Learning for Robotics

Implementing and studying machine learning methods for robotic perception, regression, classification, and autonomous decision-making.
