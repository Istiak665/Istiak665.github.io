I am a PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering at Michigan Technological University. My research focuses on computer vision, deep learning, robotics perception, and intelligent sensing for marine and ocean environments.

My work explores how visual and multimodal sensing systems can be used to understand complex environmental conditions and support autonomous marine platforms. I am particularly interested in camera-based perception, LiDAR, sensor fusion, wave and sea-state sensing, and learning-based perception systems.

## Research Interests

- Computer Vision
- Deep Learning
- Marine Robotics Perception
- Autonomous Systems
- Multimodal Sensor Fusion
- Ocean and Wave Sensing
- LiDAR and 3D Perception

## Current Research

My current research focuses on developing intelligent perception and sensing methods for marine environments. This includes learning-based estimation of ocean and wave conditions from visual data, polarimetric imaging, multimodal sensing, and perception systems for autonomous surface vehicles.

I am also interested in integrating cameras, LiDAR, IMU, GPS, sonar, and other sensors to improve environmental understanding and autonomous navigation in challenging marine environments.

## Research Themes

### Vision-Based Ocean Sensing

Developing computer vision and deep learning methods for estimating wave and sea-state information from visual observations.

### Marine Robotics Perception

Designing perception systems for autonomous marine platforms using cameras and multimodal sensors.

### Multimodal Sensing

Combining information from cameras, LiDAR, IMU, GPS, sonar, and other sensing modalities for robust environmental perception.

### Learning-Based Perception

Applying machine learning and deep learning to challenging perception problems in robotics and ocean engineering.

## Selected Research

My research includes work on camera-based significant wave height estimation, polarimetric imaging for ocean sensing, deep learning for visual wave perception, and multimodal sensing for autonomous marine systems.
