My research focuses on intelligent perception and sensing for marine and ocean environments. I am particularly interested in combining computer vision, deep learning, and multimodal sensing to understand complex environmental conditions and support autonomous marine systems.

## Research Areas

### Computer Vision for Marine Environments

Developing vision-based methods for understanding waves, sea states, and environmental conditions from camera imagery.

### Deep Learning for Environmental Perception

Designing learning-based models for regression, representation learning, visual estimation, and robust perception under challenging real-world conditions.

### Marine Robotics Perception

Developing perception systems for autonomous surface and marine platforms using visual and multimodal sensor information.

### Multimodal Sensor Fusion

Combining complementary information from cameras, LiDAR, IMU, GPS, sonar, and other sensors to improve environmental understanding and autonomous navigation.

### Ocean and Wave Sensing

Investigating image-based and sensor-based approaches for estimating wave properties, sea-state parameters, and surface dynamics.

### LiDAR and 3D Perception

Exploring LiDAR-based mapping, geometric perception, and 3D environmental understanding for autonomous systems.

## Current Research Directions

### Vision-Based Significant Wave Height Estimation

Developing deep learning methods for estimating significant wave height from coastal and marine imagery, with particular attention to rare and high sea-state conditions.

### Polarimetric Imaging for Ocean Sensing

Investigating polarization-aware visual representations and learning methods for extracting wave-related information from polarimetric imagery.

### Multisensor Marine Perception

Studying how cameras, LiDAR, IMU, GPS, sonar, and other sensing modalities can be integrated for robust environmental perception and autonomous marine operations.

### Wave Tank Experiments and Dataset Development

Designing controlled experiments to collect synchronized multimodal wave data for wave-state estimation, perception research, and autonomous marine sensing.

## Long-Term Research Goal

My long-term goal is to develop intelligent perception systems that enable autonomous robots to reliably understand and operate in complex, dynamic, and uncertain marine environments.
